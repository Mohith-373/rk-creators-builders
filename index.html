FAQ

Common questions about building your home

Can't find what you need? Send us an enquiry and we'll be happy to help.

How should I start a house construction project with you?+
Simply contact us through the enquiry form or by phone. We'll begin with a conversation about your needs, followed by a site discussion and estimate.
What areas do you serve?+
We're based in Thuraiyur, Tamil Nadu and serve homeowners in and around the surrounding area. Contact us to confirm we can support your location.
Do you handle both new builds and renovations?+
Yes. We specialise in new home construction as well as renovation, remodeling, extensions and repairs for existing homes.
How long will my project take?+
Timelines depend on the size and scope of your project. We provide a clear schedule during planning and keep you updated throughout the build.
